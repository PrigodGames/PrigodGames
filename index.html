More on
Prigod Idle.

An idle game built around short card runs and long-term progression.

Play a run when you feel like it, then come back to the resources your generators produced while you were away.

01

Card runs

Choose a route, pick cards, and build around what the run gives you.

02

Offline progress

Generators keep producing resources while the game is closed.

03

Gear and crafting

Collect equipment, improve it, and try different builds.

04

No microtransactions

Everything is earned by playing. There is no real-money shop.

Multiplayer project

Prigod
X

Five players form the hero party. A sixth player is the Overlord.

The heroes explore the dungeon together. The Overlord controls its monsters and traps and tries to stop them.
