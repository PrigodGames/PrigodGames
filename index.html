Idle
01 In development

Fantasy idle autobattler

Prigod Idle

A fantasy autobattler with idle progression and deep buildcrafting. Huge skill trees, abilities, pets, events, dungeons, and hundreds of unique items create countless builds to experiment with. Designed for PC, not mobile—and there are no microtransactions.

Seal
02 In development

Asymmetric multiplayer action

Break the Seal

A short, round-based asymmetric multiplayer game for up to six players. One player controls the dungeon as the Overlord, growing stronger every minute, while up to five heroes race to break the seals and stop them.
