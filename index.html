More on
Prigod Idle.

A deep idle autobattler built around short runs and long-term progression.

Play a run whenever you feel like it, then return to the resources your generators produced while you were away.

01

Randomized stages

Inspired by Slay the Spire: choose a route, pick cards, and build around what each run gives you.

02

Offline progress

Generators keep producing resources while the game is closed.

03

Gear and crafting

Collect and improve equipment, then experiment with unique builds. Different challenges reward different approaches.

04

No microtransactions

Everything is earned by playing. There is no real-money shop.

Prigod
X

Up to five players form the hero party; one player becomes the Overlord.
