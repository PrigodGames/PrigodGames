Privacy Policy

This policy covers this website and the games published by Prigod Games, including Prigod Idle on PC, browser and Android. A German version is available at Datenschutzerklärung.

The short version

You can play the whole game without an account and without sending anything to anyone. Saves live on your device. The only data that ever leaves your device is data you switch on yourself, and there is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics anywhere in the game or on this site.

The game: what stays on your device

Your save file, your settings and your progress are stored locally on the device you play on. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the game, or wiping the save from the in-game settings, deletes them.

The game does not ask for your name, email address or date of birth. It does not read your contacts, photos, location or files. It does not use advertising identifiers.

Online leaderboards (optional, off by default)

The game has leaderboards, an arena and a friends list. All of it is switched off until you turn on Settings → Online leaderboards and pick a display name. Until you do that, nothing is sent.

What is sent when you opt in

  • The display name you type, plus a short numeric tag so that identical names can be told apart.
  • A random ID that the game generates on your device the first time you opt in. It is not derived from your device, your hardware or any account, and it is not an advertising identifier.
  • Your scores and a snapshot of public progress values, such as level and combat power, so ladders and arena matches can be built.

Your friends list is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded.

Who processes it

Leaderboard data is stored by SilentWolf, a hosted leaderboard service running on Amazon Web Services. It receives only the data listed above.

Legal basis and retention

Consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time by turning the setting off, which stops all further submissions. Entries already posted stay on the ladder until you ask for deletion, or until that ladder is reset.

Feedback survey (optional)

The game has a feedback button. It opens a Google Forms page in your browser. To save you typing, the link carries a short technical snapshot: build type, version number, current level, prestige count, furthest level reached, combat power, which features you have used, playtime and platform.

Nothing is submitted until you press submit on that page yourself. The form does not ask for your name or email. Google is the processor for the form; Google's own privacy terms apply once you are on their page.

Legal basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Stores and platforms

Buying the game, and any achievements or cloud saves tied to a store, are handled by the store, not by us. We receive anonymous sales and refund totals, never your payment details.

  • Steam — purchases, achievements and Steam Cloud saves are handled by Valve under Valve's privacy policy.
  • Google Play — purchases are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.
  • itch.io — the browser build is hosted by itch.io under their privacy policy.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard server log data, including IP addresses, in order to serve the pages and defend against abuse. That processing is GitHub's, on the legal basis of legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds nothing from third parties. Videos and images are served from the site itself. Links to YouTube, TikTok, Steam or GitHub are plain links: nothing loads from those companies until you click through.

Deleting your data

To have a leaderboard entry and its stored progress values removed, email prigodgames@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the display name you play under, including the number tag shown next to it in game (for example Hero#01234). That name is the only thing that identifies the record, so it has to be exact.

Requests are answered within 30 days. There is nothing to pay and no account to log into.

You can also do most of it yourself: turning off Settings → Online leaderboards stops all submissions, and wiping the save from the settings screen removes the local data, including the friends list and the random ID.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its processing restricted, to data portability, and to object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting what was lawful before.

Use the email address above for any of these. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. The competent one here is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland, Saarbrücken.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and no data is knowingly collected from them. There is no advertising and no targeted content of any kind. If you believe a child has posted a leaderboard entry, write to the address above and it will be removed.
