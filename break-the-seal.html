Break
the Seal.

A short, round-based asymmetric multiplayer game for up to six players. One player controls the dungeon as the Overlord, growing stronger every minute, while up to five heroes race to break the seals and stop them.

How it plays

Multiplayer project

One dungeon,
two sides.

Up to five players form the hero party; one player becomes the Overlord.

The heroes work together to destroy seals and confront the Overlord. Meanwhile, the Overlord controls monsters, lays traps, and gathers power to eliminate the heroes.
